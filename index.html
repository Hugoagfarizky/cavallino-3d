02 The heart

Naturally aspirated.
Utterly unfiltered.

Four and a half litres, no turbochargers, 127 cv per litre. International Engine of the Year, built in Maranello's foundry.

A

Flat-plane crankshaft

Evenly spaced exhaust pulses for razor-sharp throttle response and the 9,000 rpm scream.

B

Rosso intake plenums

Variable-geometry intakes in wrinkle-finish red, tuned for ram effect in every gear.

C

200-bar direct injection

Multi-hole injectors allow a 12.5:1 compression ratio without knock.

D

Equal-length headers

Hydroformed manifolds turn combustion into one continuous harmonic note.

04 Heritage

Built for the track,
bred for the road.

“Aerodynamics are for people who can't build engines.” Enzo Ferrari
  1. 1947

    The first Cavallino

    The 125 S takes to the streets of Piacenza with Franco Cortese at the wheel.

  2. 1975

    The first mid-engine V8

    The 308 GTB introduces the transverse 2.9 litre V8 and a lineage that leads to the 458.

  3. 2009

    458 Italia

    Developed with Michael Schumacher's input, and the last naturally aspirated mid-engine V8 Ferrari.

  4. Today

    A collector's icon

    Widely held to be one of the greatest sounding road cars ever made.
